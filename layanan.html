Tahap 01 dari 06

Konsultasi & Perencanaan

A clear, structured process for delivering quality work.

  1. 01

    Konsultasi & Perencanaan

    Define the project scope, budget, schedule, and client vision.

  2. 02

    Desain & Anggaran

    Develop concepts, select materials, and finalize budgets and schedules.

  3. 03

    Perizinan & Persetujuan

    Mengurus perizinan dan persetujuan agar proyek memenuhi peraturan yang berlaku.

  4. 04

    Pelaksanaan Konstruksi

    Coordinate workers, suppliers, and execution with a focus on safety and quality.

  5. 05

    Pengendalian Mutu

    Conduct thorough inspections to ensure work meets required standards and specifications.

  6. 06

    Serah Terima & Dukungan

    Melakukan peninjauan akhir, serah terima, dan tindak lanjut pasca-konstruksi sesuai kesepakatan.
